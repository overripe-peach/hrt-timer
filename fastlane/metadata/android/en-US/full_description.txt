HRT Timer is an android widget that is a medicine dose timer designed around the needs of people on HRT.  
Use the settings page to set the medicine label, the timer duration in days, and a desired background color for the dose ready indicator state.
When you take your dose, simply press the widget.  That sets the button to a grey disabled state. When it is time for your next dose the widget will change
back to the ready state color (defaults to green).  No more messing about with calendar events or trying to remember when you took your last dose.
You can have multiple widgets on the phone.  Data from one widget instance will not cross over with another.  

If you have feature requests or bug reports please drop them in the github - https://github.com/overripe-peach/hrt-timer
